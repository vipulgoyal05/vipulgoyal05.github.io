01 / About

Curiosity at the
cellular and systems level.

I am interested in understanding how the brain responds to injury and disease, and how cellular processes might be harnessed to restore function.

My research interests centre on neuroregeneration — particularly the relationship between neural systems, stem cells, and tissue repair.

I am also drawn to the emerging intersection of cancer and neuroscience: how neural activity, tumour cells, and the surrounding microenvironment interact.

I enjoy connecting biological questions with experimental methods, quantitative thinking, and careful interpretation of data.

Questions I want to explore

  • How does the brain respond to traumatic injury?
  • What cellular mechanisms support neural repair?
  • How can stem cells contribute to regeneration?
  • How do nerves and neural signals influence cancer biology?
  • How can molecular and behavioural data be connected?

RESEARCH PROJECT
IISER PUNE

NeuroSCAPE Lab

Traumatic brain injury · Hippocampal focus

Working on a mouse model of traumatic brain injury using controlled cortical impact (CCI), with a focus on hippocampal injury and post-trauma behavioural changes.

  • Assessing behavioural changes using the Open Field Test (OFT) and Elevated Zero Maze (EZM), before and after injury.
  • Working with a sham and injury comparison framework.
  • Project includes histological analysis of brain tissue.

04 / Featured project

A closer look at my current work.

Ongoing research TBI · CCI · Hippocampus

Hippocampal injury and behavioural outcomes after traumatic brain injury

This project investigates the effects of traumatic brain injury in a mouse model, with particular interest in the hippocampus and behavioural changes following injury. The study combines a controlled cortical impact model with behavioural assessment and tissue analysis.

Model Mouse CCI model of TBI
Region of interest Hippocampus
Behavioural assays OFT and EZM

The project is ongoing. Results and conclusions will be added when the analysis is complete.
