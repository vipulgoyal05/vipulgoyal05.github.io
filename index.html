01 / The person

A little about me.

I am pursuing a BS-MS degree at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune, with a deep interest in understanding biological systems.

What draws me to biology is its ability to connect seemingly different scales of life — from molecules and cells to tissues, organs and the behaviour of an organism.

I am particularly interested in how cells respond to their environment, how tissues repair themselves, and how these processes become altered in disease.

My scientific curiosity Neuroscience · Stem Cell Biology · Cancer Biology
