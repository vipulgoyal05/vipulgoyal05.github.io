01 / About

Curiosity, guided
by biology.

I am a biology student interested in understanding the mechanisms that shape the nervous system — from development and plasticity to injury, degeneration, and repair.

My research curiosity lies in connecting cellular biology with complex neural phenomena. I am particularly drawn to how stem cells and their surrounding environments influence tissue regeneration and disease.

I enjoy working at the interface of experimental biology, neuroscience, and translational research, where fundamental questions can eventually inform therapeutic possibilities.

A question that drives me How can we understand the cellular and molecular processes that determine whether neural tissue adapts, degenerates, or regenerates after injury or disease?
