01 / The person

A little about me.

I am pursuing a BS-MS dual degree in Biological Sciences at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune.

My curiosity lies in understanding how biological systems work — from the molecular interactions inside a cell to the complex architecture of the brain.

I am particularly drawn to neuroscience, stem cell biology and cancer research. I enjoy exploring how cells respond to injury, how tissues recover, and how these processes become altered in disease.

For me, research is an opportunity to turn curiosity into questions, and questions into experiments.

A scientific curiosity

What makes living systems so extraordinary?

The ability of cells to communicate, adapt, differentiate and sometimes regenerate continues to fascinate me.
